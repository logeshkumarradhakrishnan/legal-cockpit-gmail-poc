Legal Email Classification
1.	Purpose
This document lists the keyword sets and classification intent used to automatically sort incoming Outlook emails into aviation-related legal case categories. Emails matching personal-matter keywords (e.g. the user's home loan) are excluded from classification entirely and never filed as legal cases.
2. Classification Logic Summary
1.	Fetch email (subject, sender, date, body, attachment) from Outlook.
2.	Check against the Personal Exclusion list - if matched, discard and do not classify.
3.	AI based categorization based on keyword and intent - assign the category based on best fit.
4.	Log the result (category + classification method) to the output file for review.
5.	The agent runs once every three days to automatically classify incoming emails and identify/filter emails relevant to Legal.

5. External Stakeholder Registry 
A maintained, structured registry (domain/email → stakeholder name → category → typical nature of correspondence) forms the backbone of Signal Layer 1. Seeded at build time and kept current by the Legal team. 
Email domains for each of these + any additional stakeholders need to be collected basis discussions with GM+ 
A. Aviation Sector Regulators (Domestic) 
•	DGCA (Directorate General of Civil Aviation) - CARs, airworthiness, licensing, safety circulars 
•	BCAS (Bureau of Civil Aviation Security) - security compliance, AVSEC circulars, security audits 
•	AAI (Airports Authority of India) - ATC coordination, navigation services, land/lease matters 
•	AERA (Airports Economic Regulatory Authority) - tariff orders, consultation papers, economic regulation 
•	TDSAT
•	Ministry of Civil Aviation (MoCA) - policy directives 
•	Airport Health Organization (APHO) - public health/quarantine notices 
B. International Aviation & Regulatory Agencies 
•	ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization) - State Letters, SARPs (Standards and Recommended Practices) notifications, USOAP audit correspondence, safety directives 
•	IATA (International Air Transport Association) - IOSA audit findings, operational/commercial standards, IATA Clearing House matters, dispute correspondence 
•	ACI (Airports Council International) / ACI Asia-Pacific - accreditation, benchmarking, advisory correspondence 
•	WHO (World Health Organization) - International Health Regulations (IHR), public health emergency notifications 
•	WCO (World Customs Organization) - international customs standards correspondence, where applicable 
•	Foreign civil aviation authorities / bilateral air services correspondence - where a matter involves a foreign state or carrier 
C. Central Government / Statutory Bodies 
•	Customs Department (Bengaluru Customs) - cargo, international passenger operations 
•	Bureau of Immigration (BOI) / FRRO 
•	Income Tax Department - assessment orders, demand notices, TDS matters 
•	CBIC / GST authorities (central and state) 
•	Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) / Registrar of Companies (ROC) 
•	Competition Commission of India (CCI) 
•	Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) 
•	PESO (Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation) - fuel farm compliance 
•	FSSAI - food safety at airport F&B outlets 
•	Legal Metrology Department - weights & measures (retail, fuel dispensing) 
•	Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) - environmental clearances 
•	National Green Tribunal (NGT) 
D. State / Local Government Bodies (Karnataka-specific) 
•	Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB) 
•	Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board (KIADB) - land acquisition/allotment matters 
•	BESCOM (electricity) 
•	Devanahalli Town Municipal Council / Gram Panchayat (local municipal authority) 
•	Deputy Commissioner, Bengaluru Rural District - revenue/land records 
•	Karnataka State Fire and Emergency Services - Fire NOC 
•	Karnataka Labour Department / Chief Labour Commissioner 
•	Karnataka State Human Rights Commission 
E. Judicial / Quasi-Judicial Bodies 
•	High Court of Karnataka, District Courts, Consumer Courts 
•	NCLT (if corporate matters arise) 
•	Labour Courts / Industrial Tribunals 
•	Arbitral Tribunals / Arbitration Institutions 
•	e-Courts system (ecourts.gov.in) - case status, hearing notices 
F. Law Firms / External Counsel 
•	Both BIAL’s retained counsel and opposing counsel - maintained as a curated domain list. Opposing-counsel emails are treated as highest priority, since they typically signal active or threatened litigation. 
G. Contractual / Commercial Counterparties (Vendors) 
•	Airlines (contractual notices, SLA breach claims) 
•	Ground handling agencies 
•	Retail/F&B concessionaires 
•	Cargo operators 
•	Into-plane fuel suppliers 
•	Construction/O&M contractors and vendors 
•	Insurance companies/surveyors (claims correspondence) 
H. Passengers / General Public 
•	Direct passenger complaints escalated to a GM+ mailbox (email, or forwarded from a web-form submission) 
•	Complaints escalated internally from the Customer Experience / Guest Relations team 
•	Complaints escalated from the Chairman’s / MD’s office 
•	Complaints forwarded from the social media / PR team 
•	Consumer forum notices (District/State/National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission) - treated as a formal legal proceeding, not a complaint 
•	Disability rights / advocacy organizations writing on a passenger’s behalf 
I. Media 
•	Journalists and media houses - direct queries or forwarded from Corporate Communications/PR 
•	Media house legal/compliance teams (treated as external counsel correspondence)
